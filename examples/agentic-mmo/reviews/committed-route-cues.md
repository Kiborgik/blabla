## Delivery facts and exact binding

A delivery cue requires a `trade-delivered` event with a canonical revision/index ID. Resource details come from the retained immutable agreement only when the exact route ID, offer ID in the engine's cause, actor/partner, accepted/delivered/closed round bounds and recorded aggregate cargo all match. The aggregate is parsed only from one recognized finite cargo effect. Its sum uses the same resource order as `resolveTrade`.

The two cargo vectors, consumed upkeep vectors and credited commerce vectors stay separate and cloned. Construction cost is not counted again. `factSource` states whether those facts were reconstructed from the bound agreement or only the event aggregate is available. This is not a self-contained historical delivery receipt.

A later stopped agreement, later failed attempt or current route damage does not erase the earlier successful result. Missing, replaced, illustrative or inconsistent bindings retain only the recorded aggregate and identifiers. A correctly bound agreement with an unresolved endpoint retains exact town IDs and payment facts, but has no map anchor. An unrelated capital is never substituted. Zero cargo cannot earn a delivery accent.

The helper depends on the existing canonical agreement immutability and validated event input. It is not a second world validator. Future changes to the current prose event grammar will conservatively fall back to aggregate-only or unknown facts until the shared projection is updated. A later durable structured delivery receipt would remove that dependency; it is not necessary to make the present fallback honest.

## Public repair facts

A repair cue requires the structured result's outcome and project ID to agree with the event type and detail. Its exact route/agreement and participant pair determine whether current endpoints and current damage may be attached. The charter proposer can be the trade recipient, so payer identity comes from the result rather than route direction.

The cloned result retains actual contributions, outcome/reason and damage before/after. The displayed reduction is the actual subtraction, including a repair clamped at zero, rather than the catalogue's maximum. Current damage remains a separate field and can legitimately be higher after a later disruption. A missing or replaced agreement retains the historical result without borrowing its successor's endpoints.

Only completed positive repair with both exact towns earns a repair accent. Cancellation, refusal, expiry, failure, no-work-needed and zero/unknown reduction remain factual non-motion outcomes. Neither commitment nor blocked trade creates a result cue. `resolvePublicProjects` runs its own repair/payment operation; this helper never turns that operation into a shipment. Later delivery must have its own committed event.
