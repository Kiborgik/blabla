## Executed boundary

`LandTransportProbes` is separate from the durable sequential campaign and every existing probe. Construction, reset and an untouched observation perform no geographic fixture initialization. The first selected geographic action lazily builds a fixed seed-1701 baseline through actual registered releases. That baseline is cached and cloned, never exposed by reference. Invalid or huge fixture selectors map to the same bounded initial fixture; they never control iteration or allocation sizes.

The seven adapter actions now call real engine operations. A proposal is a real exact-city action; recipient consent is the exact `applyCommand` result. Positive consent and due fixtures contain no stock, technology, city, era, milestone or map edits. The Ember–Pelagos quote has ten edges and cadence five. Consent at round zero first permits delivery at round five. The due fixture reaches round four through actual strategy catchup, then resolution executes the entire next economy beat.

Full-beat payment is measured against a second branch that legally stops the same agreement before that beat. This preserves ordinary production and upkeep on both branches. Exact civilization-resource differences are compared with the immutable cargo, upkeep and yield vectors, separately for both parties and every resource. Delivery requires the actual delivery counter increment and emitted event. Refused commands compare complete world state and empty events; blocked exchanges compare resources with the stopped control. There is no authored success flag and no replica shipment implementation.

Quote validity uses the canonical planner and passability helpers; cadence uses the shared cadence helper and recipe data. This binding therefore connects executable contracts to actual runtime helpers, while independent topology/planner correctness remains covered by the geographic engine tests. Historical behavior is compared against actual old-release consent, rather than a second arithmetic implementation of its schedule. A round-two abstract offer accepted after release keeps first due round four and exactly replays after JSON serialization.

## Adversarial fixtures and temporal facts

Fixture 3 changes one quoted tile to mountain after legal proposal. Fixture 4 makes the same edit after legal consent. Fixture 5 sets recipient materials to zero and city damage to 100 after legal consent and catchup, suppressing production that could otherwise hide the shortage. Fixture 7 sets route damage to its retained disabling threshold. These are labeled synthetic component adversaries, not legal depletion or live agent play. Fixture 6 is the unedited early accepted agreement.

Reviewing these real cases exposed two prospective-rule omissions, reported to the orchestrator rather than hidden by favorable observations:

- Funded stock and a passable quote do not imply a usable route. `land_route_usable` separately reports retained agreement enablement, exact owned endpoints and damage below its retained threshold.
- Next-beat eligibility differs from the prior executed beat. `land_attempt_due` always describes the current next beat; `land_last_attempt_due` derives from the captured pre-resolution agreement. Repeated resolve calls therefore cannot reuse stale positive due facts.

No agent identity, live action receipt or model provenance is manufactured. Test commands are deterministic local fixtures with an explicit fixed epoch.
