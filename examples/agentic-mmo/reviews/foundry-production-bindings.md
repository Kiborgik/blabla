## Isolation and finite work

Construction and observation of an unused probe execute no journey. Unknown actions throw before initializing or changing it. First use lazily computes one private legal basis and retains it for that instance. Every selected transition is replayed from a detached exact before/input. Reset clears observable execution state while retaining only that immutable private basis; it does not carry a mutated world into the next action. Two instances have independent basis/state, and snapshot mutation cannot change either probe.

Development timing is diagnostic, not a wall-clock acceptance gate: first action3721ms including the686-frame basis; exact completion9ms, work7ms, block14ms, pause27ms, deferred extraction4ms, shipment5ms, refusal20ms, full replay2425ms. Five cases took19.63seconds. Bounds belong to this seed1701 legal fixture, exact city-1 recipient/city-0 donor, route-338, current cards and one Foundry. These measurements do not establish arbitrary empire performance, global adapter request cost or service query budgets.
