These statements do not yet form an explicit implementation-ready account of the newly approved hybrid model.

**Fix:** reconcile mission/priorities, goals, design and roadmap into four layers: fast tactical actions, slower strategic consequences, the independent six-minute calendar baseline, and deliberate hybrid history advancement. Author a separately scoped milestone operation before implementing it: eligible source feature/event, causal identifier, visible reason/delta, revision protection and exactly-once replay. Preserve baseline elapsed-tick accounting; routine consultation/review still contributes zero years unless an explicit authorized milestone is selected. Test duplicate cause rejection and a milestone interleaved with due baseline ticks. Retain current clock-only evidence as truthful current-stage behavior rather than claiming hybrid is already implemented. Do not change the runtime or tests merely to make old prose pass.
