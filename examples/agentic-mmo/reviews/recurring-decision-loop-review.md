## Inspected behavior

- `decisionObligations` derives Required, Active and Blocked from the shared structural eligibility helpers. Overdue tactical admission does not erase requirements. Research selection does not require the full completion science balance. Exact-town construction requires an affordable start; exhausted or gated pools retain concrete blockers.
- `decisionActionBlock` refuses Hold while an actionable lane remains, rejects replacing an active research target, and preserves an active town order. Emergency repair, consent and other legal tactics remain available. This is the agreed bounded finish/hold rule, not proof that an actor will prioritize requirements over every optional tactic.
- Construction records the accepted revision identity, exact town/building, rule version, paid vector and work requirement. It pays once and installs nothing at start. Opening work uses the shared city productivity function. Economy captures that work before supplies, growth, trade, repair projects and discoveries; completion is committed at the end.
- Installation, one-time effects and the operation record appear only at completion. Paid recipes first receive an operating allocation on a later beat. An active order has no same-beat production or second automatic queue entry.
- A retained completion is historical. The resulting observation can immediately require the next choice. The two-item action plan remains a nonexecuting intention; completing the build command means starting its order, not completing its construction.
- Another actor and shared strategic production continue while an absent civilization has open decisions. The release preserves old instant-build and research-switch histories and creates no retroactive orders.
- Canonical validation rejects malformed/foreign orders, duplicate identities, invalid work/progress, future chronology and inconsistent completed buildings. It allows missing new fields in older states and historical paid/work values that differ from later catalogue tuning. Last-completion references survive bounded event-feed eviction; validation does not pretend it independently authenticates an evicted event's original text.
- The existing repository atomically commits candidate upkeep, the exact action, history and one command receipt. An invalid post-catchup Hold rolls back all candidate work; explicit upkeep remains usable. Strict composite CAS and ordinary exact-action rebasing retain their existing distinct boundaries.
