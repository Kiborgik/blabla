The largest intentionally concentrates decision lanes in one actor rather than spreading them across five seats. Land ownership, ample resources, known agriculture and low opening wellbeing are synthetic setup, accepted by the actual canonical validator. They are not a legal founding journey or live agent history.

After setup, actual release-gated engine commands choose civic research and two consecutive construction cycles, farm then workshop, in every owned town. All inputs remain immutable; each command/catchup is repeated against canonical JSON restoration and compared exactly. Completed results validate. Two complete workload runs reproduce the same initial/final hashes, counts and payloads. Recorded transitions include harmless catchup calls that may perform no beat; they are not all state-changing commands.

Five snapshots cover idle, first orders, first completions, second orders alongside retained first receipts, and second completions. Each full and compact observation preserves exactly the same ordered actions; each detached tree projection receives no legal-option list and therefore does not enumerate the trade menu. The five-town active snapshots happen to land on a due-strategy boundary and truthfully have zero actions; this is recorded rather than interpreted as truncation. The largest ready-menu snapshot is included in every maximum below.

## Work counts and recommendation

For N owned towns, decision derivation returns N construction lanes plus one research lane. Source inspection gives an upper bound of 9 technology and 8×N building eligibility evaluations when all lanes are empty: 17, 49 and 209 here. These are source-derived call bounds, not sampled CPU counters; nested scans are not claimed constant-time. Active lanes avoid those catalogue loops. Construction snapshots opening work once and resolves in stable city order at economy end. The test verifies bounded retained objects and actual completion counts rather than installing instrumentation in production.

The measured recurring objects remain small: 4,743 bytes for 25 completion receipts plus one research receipt. The larger payload is the complete 753-option compact menu, and the no-option spectator tree avoids that enumeration. The 25-town decision facts are about 7.9% of the largest compact payload. There is no evidence here warranting a new cache, duplicate legality path or deferred mechanics shipment. Keep the canonical projection and bounded records. If larger actor menus become a real transport bottleneck, reuse the separately accepted paging seam and measure its actual wrappers; do not silently drop choices. A later bounded study could factor repeated blocker facts if their measured share grows materially.

This establishes a source-local resource baseline. The actual recurring choice→progress→completion→next-choice gameplay and spectator comprehension outcome remains separately open under the owner goal.
