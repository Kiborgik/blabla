# Operating and strategic probe review

Actual reviewer: gpt-6-astra, 2026-10-16. Reviewed `cli/operations-probes.ts`, adapter bindings, the production/strategy contracts and their focused tests. No production source was changed. No bounded blocker was found for these stated probe domains.

## What the operating probe establishes

`createSyntheticOperatingWorld` explicitly constructs a controlled irrigation fixture after applying actual supported releases. It inserts a prebuilt irrigation structure and one operation's material stock; it does not claim to have researched or constructed that building through player actions. Its technologies remain empty, which the tests explicitly assert. This is component evidence, not a valid historical construction journey or actual development-agent play.

The allocator action invokes the real `allocateOperations`. Observation projects the real current toggle, recipe availability, paid/produced receipt and material reserve. The recipe guard limits this debit projection to the authored single-material irrigation input, so summing its paid vector and comparing material cents has a defined meaning. The probe does not establish multi-resource atomicity for every recipe or once-per-beat command idempotency. Separate engine and persistence evidence must support those broader claims.

The full production action invokes real timestamped `catchUpStrategy`, advances its own one-minute clock and retains the actual returned events. It does not directly call economy while pretending that round/scheduler counters advanced. Pause goes through the real exact-city `applyCommand`. Mixed component allocation, actual scheduled production and pause remain canonical according to `isWorldState`; no round or scheduler field is manually repaired afterward. Failed repeated pauses preserve the world.

`supplied-working-recipe-produces` is honestly limited to this undamaged, positive-output irrigation fixture. No probe action introduces damage, and the existing productivity formula remains positive in this fixture even at low wellbeing. It is not a universal claim that every funded recipe always has positive output: a different recipe or fully damaged city may legitimately pay and produce nothing. The inactive/blocked checks use receipt input/output, not total stock movement, because unrelated city production may change materials during the same beat. Initial material exhaustion supplies a non-vacuous blocked case; pause supplies the inactive case.

## What the strategic probe establishes

The strategy fixture starts with actual compatible releases and an actual proposer command. Recipient consent is submitted through normal legality, timing and revision validation. A successful acceptance creates an enabled agreement with immutable terms and a strictly later delivery round, while delivery count and route volume remain zero. Repeated or expired acceptance is refused. No recipient consent or completed shipment is installed directly into fixture state.

Strategic beats call real `catchUpStrategy`. The focused witness checks every beat before the first due date, then compares the resulting state to an independently invoked engine sequence and requires the actual delivery count, positive route volume and `trade-delivered` event. An unrelated actor's wait does not deliver cargo. This establishes the immediate-order/later-commitment distinction for the tested agreement. It does not prove every network topology, all shortage or cancellation cases, unattended host wake timing, live deployment or live agent negotiation.
