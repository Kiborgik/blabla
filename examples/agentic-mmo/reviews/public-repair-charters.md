## Consent and immutable commitment

The proposal binds an exact existing route and its original accepted offer ID. Only a route participant can propose, only the named recipient can accept or decline, and acceptance rechecks the target, both civic prerequisites and both complete contribution vectors. Proposal and acceptance collect and reserve nothing. The compact choice text explicitly warns that later spending can prevent fulfillment.

The monetary, timing and repair terms are cloned into the proposal and retained on commitment. The current recipe supplies eligibility requirements; this does not authorize retuning previously released prerequisites. One pending or committed project is allowed per unordered civilization pair, with a total active cap of ten. Exact action shapes and current legality are shared with the bounded actor planner; no proposal, consent or payment is automatically chosen for an agent.

Pending consent expires after four strategic rounds and is revalidated at strategic beats. A committed repair is due two rounds after acceptance. It remains cancellable by either participant before resolution, including after the trade agreement stops. Committed target failure is resolved at its due beat, rather than silently cancelling on an unrelated tactical action. A stopped or replaced agreement, missing endpoint or lost civic prerequisite cannot transfer the original charter to a new agreement.

## Delayed fulfillment and economic ordering

At the due beat, both complete snapshotted resource vectors are checked before either is debited. A failed or otherwise unfulfilled project pays nothing, emits a reason and leaves the active set; there is no automatic retry. Successful fulfillment pays two materials and one energy from each partner, then reduces only the exact route's damage by at most 25, clamped at zero. A partly repaired route still requires the agreed full contribution. A route already at zero damage terminates without payment.

The resolver runs after ordinary production, household costs and trade. Thus same-beat income may fund a due charter. This is intentionally distinct from the opening-stock funding boundary for paid building recipes. Trade attempted earlier in the due beat does not retroactively succeed after repair. Project completion does not create cargo, change agreement consent, increase delivery counters or rewrite shipment history. Terminal receipts distinguish contributions paid, actual before/after damage and the original agreement identity.

Projects are resolved in stable ID order. When several due projects share scarce resources, an earlier fully funded project can leave a later project unfunded; the later failure cannot take a partial contribution from its other partner. This is deterministic allocation, not a claim of balanced political incentives.

## Genuine legal journey

The main journey starts from an ordinary seeded world and uses registered compatible releases, canonical legal choices and actual strategic catch-up. It does not edit resources, technology, eras, milestones, institutions or routes to manufacture eligibility. It reaches Cultivation, farms, Civic institutions, three accepted and subsequently operating bilateral routes, the state milestone and both required gathering places. Two legal earthquake events damage the route, with actual strategic time satisfying the event cooldown.

The journey verifies that proposal and acceptance leave civilization stocks and the route unchanged. On the due beat, an actual blocked trade precedes completed repair from 50 to 25 damage, with both exact contributions recorded. No delivery is created by that repair. A later eligible strategic beat delivers cargo. Repeating an already realized strategic timestamp changes nothing. Each accepted journey transition preserves its input, validates its canonical state and gives the same result after JSON restoration.

Other legal scenarios cover refusal by outsiders and the wrong participant, explicit decline, both cancellation rights, expiry, stopping trade after commitment and invalidating a follow-up acceptance after its target is already committed. These are deterministic engine witnesses, not online agent play or hosted transport evidence.

The tests separately label their detached-state scarcity and corruption fixtures. They exercise either partner lacking a due input, same-beat income funding fulfillment, missing or replaced agreement targets, removed civic prerequisites, an already repaired route, a partially repaired route and competing projects sharing one scarce balance. Validator probes cover malformed active records and terminal receipts. Historical snapshot terms may legitimately differ from today's catalogue; validation checks their bounded shape and status accounting rather than silently rewriting history. These checks do not prove that an arbitrary externally forged historical receipt was generated by an old binary. ## Remaining release and presentation limits

The registered charter successor adds the public-project capability; it does not activate itself in the inhabited foundations world. Owner permission for live capability activation remains pending. The legal witness does not establish live political behavior, balance, renderer fidelity or physical terrain navigation. Existing trade routes are logical exchanges between agreed towns, so a schematic route or repair cue must not imply a traversed land road.

The next useful connected increment is the already proposed exact source-town and recipient-town choice, with immutable accepted endpoints and compact geography facts. That successor should preserve historical first-town actions in older releases and keep any future terrain path or travel-time mechanic separately explicit. No broader design expansion is needed to accept this bounded charter implementation.
