## Accepted design boundaries

Reuse the [connected catalogue](content-design/CONNECTED_CATALOGUE.md) and [structured input](content-design/catalogue.json). The baseline is Civilization VI with selected Gathering Storm mechanisms; numeric quantities are original provisional tuning.

Local Power is a same-town, same-beat resource. A staffed plant pays two opening Coal for eight Power and four receipt-only emissions; a staffed Factory consumes three Power for twelve base Materials under existing output modifiers. Unused Power expires. Stored Energy, later extraction and incoming cargo cannot fund the opening generator. Server validates bounded historical receipts; Art distinguishes the latest attempt from the last productive result.

The proposed Factory project-work bonus remains deferred with Core and Creative until its applicable project family and modifier order are specified. No Power change advances the calendar or invents a milestone.
