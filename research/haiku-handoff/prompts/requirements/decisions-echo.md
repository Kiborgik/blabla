
## Echo decisions

- Echo requires equal phases while transfer requires different phases; the two rules are
  deliberately opposite and must not be copied from one to the other.
- The quarantined-target condition is the same as transfer's (source in phase 0). Combined
  with equal phases, echo into a quarantined vault happens only with both vaults in phase 0,
  which a quarantined vault reaches after a restart or after a return rotation with odd
  charge 5 or less.
- Echo sums resonance without a cap; charges are never touched by echo.
- A quarantined source cannot echo; a sealed vault can neither echo nor receive.
